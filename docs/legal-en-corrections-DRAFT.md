> We may collect **non-personal information** automatically when you visit our site, including your browser type, device information, IP address, and browsing behaviour. We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyse site traffic.

**After:**
> When you visit Kit Finder, some technical data is processed automatically, such as your **IP address**, browser type and device information. We treat your IP address as **personal data**. **Before** (§1, 2nd paragraph):
> We do **not** collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details unless you voluntarily provide them (e.g., through a contact form).

## 3. Google Analytics (GA4), cookie consent and Advanced Search (Privacy §3)

**Before** (§3 Cookies):
> Our website uses **cookies** to remember your preferences, such as currency selection and favourites. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect some functionality of the site.

**After:**
> **3. Cookies and Analytics**
> **Preferences.** Kit Finder stores your choices (cookie choice, country and currency, language, favourites) in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us and are needed for the site to work.
> **Analytics (Google Analytics 4).** With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics is **only loaded after you click "Accept"** on the cookie banner; if you click "Reject", it is not loaded. When it is active, it records which pages you visit, the searches you type, which store links you click and, in Advanced Search, the filters you choose (team, player, size, decade, season, version, sleeve and number of results). Text typed in the Advanced Search team box is sent in lower case, cut to 40 characters, and **not sent at all** if it looks like an email address or a phone number. Google acts as our [processor/provider — LAWYER] and may process data in [the United States — LAWYER].
> **Changing your choice.** [You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page — this link DOES NOT EXIST yet; needs a small code change, or remove this sentence.]
> **Store cookies.** When you click through to a store, that store or its affiliate network may set its own cookies under its own policy.
