Why long sleeve shirts are so collectible

Many of football's most famous European nights — foggy autumn Champions League fixtures, wet Cup finals, cold-weather derbies — were played in long sleeve kits, which is part of why certain long sleeve jerseys carry more nostalgia than their short sleeve equivalents from the same season.

Long sleeve shirts were also standard issue for many goalkeepers throughout football history, meaning collectors searching for iconic goalkeeper jerseys will find long sleeve construction far more common than short sleeve.

Because long sleeve versions were typically produced in smaller quantities than the standard short sleeve kit for any given season, original examples in good condition are often scarcer — and more valuable — than their short sleeve counterparts.
