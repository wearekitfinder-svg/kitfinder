The most collectible Inter Milan kits

The 2009-10 Treble-winning shirt — worn by Diego Milito, Wesley Sneijder and Samuel Eto'o when José Mourinho's side won Serie A, the Coppa Italia and the Champions League in the same season — is the definitive modern Inter jersey.

Ronaldo Nazário's shirts from his 1997-2002 spell at the club, cut short by injury but studded with moments of brilliance, remain some of the most desirable Inter shirts on the vintage market.

The 2002-03 Champions League semi-final shirt, from the famous all-Milan tie against AC Milan, is popular among collectors of the Derby della Madonnina rivalry.

Shirts featuring long-time sponsor Pirelli, the Milan-based tyre manufacturer with historic ties to the club, are a recurring and recognisable thread through Inter's shirt history.

Inter Milan kits by decade

  • 1980s: Inter's shirts of this era favoured understated branding, a legacy of the club's traditionally low-key approach to shirt design compared to its rivals.
  • 1990s: Nike became kit supplier in 1998, arriving in time for Ronaldo's spell and the 1998 UEFA Cup final win over Lazio.
  • 2000s: The Nike era continued through the 2010 Treble season under José Mourinho, the club's greatest modern achievement.
  • 2010s-2020s: Nike has remained Inter's long-term kit partner, with recent shirts marking the club's return to Champions League finals under Simone Inzaghi.
