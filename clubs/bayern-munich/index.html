The most collectible Bayern Munich kits

The 1970s Adidas era — covering Bayern's three consecutive European Cup wins (1974, 1975, 1976) — is the golden age for Bayern collectors. Kits featuring Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller and Sepp Maier are the rarest and most valuable shirts in German football history.

The Opel-sponsored kits of the 1980s and 1990s are the heart of most Bayern collections. These Adidas designs — worn by Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, Lothar Matthäus and later Oliver Kahn — are classic German football shirts.

The 2000-01 Champions League winning shirt (penalty shootout against Valencia in Milan) is the most popular modern Bayern jersey. Worn by Oliver Kahn, Stefan Effenberg and Giovane Élber.

The 2012-13 treble-winning shirt — Robben's winner against Dortmund at Wembley — is the most sought-after recent Bayern shirt. Any shirt from this historic domestic and European treble season is popular with modern collectors.

Bayern Munich kits by decade

  • 1970s: Adidas supplied Bayern through their European Cup hat-trick. These shirts are extraordinarily rare.
  • 1980s-90s: Adidas and Opel produced some iconic kits. The 1995-96 Champions League final shirt (lost to Juventus) is sought after.
  • 2000s: Adidas continue. The 2000-01 Champions League winning kit is the standout from this era.
  • 2010s-present: Adidas remain. The 2013 treble and 2020 sextuple shirts are the most collected recent Bayern kits.
