The most collectible Sevilla kits

The 2005-06 UEFA Cup final shirt, worn by Frédéric Kanouté and Julio Baptista when Sevilla thrashed Middlesbrough 4-0 in Eindhoven, marked the start of the club's golden era of European pedigree.

Shirts from Sevilla's back-to-back Europa League wins of 2014 and 2015 under Unai Emery are widely collected, part of a run that also included further titles in 2016 and 2020 — six Europa League/UEFA Cup titles in total, a competition record.

Kanouté's shirts throughout his time at the club remain fan favourites, as the Malian striker became one of the most beloved foreign players in Sevilla's history.

Older shirts from Sevilla's 2006-07 Copa del Rey and Spanish Super Cup winning campaigns, completing a historic treble alongside the UEFA Cup, are sought after by collectors who followed that remarkable squad.

Sevilla kits by decade

  • 1980s-90s: Sevilla moved between several kit suppliers during a period that included relegation and promotion, with the club yet to establish its modern European identity.
  • 2000s: Puma supplied Sevilla's kit through the historic UEFA Cup-winning seasons of 2006 and 2007 under Juande Ramos.
  • 2010s: Sevilla's back-to-back-to-back Europa League wins of 2014, 2015 and 2016 under Unai Emery cemented the club as Europe's dominant force in the competition.
  • 2020s: A sixth Europa League title in 2020, won behind closed doors in Cologne during the pandemic, extended Sevilla's unmatched record in the competition.
