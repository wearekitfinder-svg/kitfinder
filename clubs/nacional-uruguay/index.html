Nacional (Uruguay) Vintage & Retro Football Shirts

Club Nacional de Football's white shirt with its blue-and-red diagonal tricolor sash was founded in 1899 specifically to represent Uruguayan-born players, in contrast to the British-founded clubs of the era — a distinction still central to the club's identity today. The most collectible Nacional kits

The 1980 Intercontinental Cup shirt, worn when Nacional beat Nottingham Forest to become world champions, is one of the club's proudest and most historically significant jerseys.

Shirts from Nacional's earlier 1971 Intercontinental Cup and Copa Libertadores-winning campaign, and the club's third Libertadores title in 1988, are treasured by long-time supporters tracing the club's continental pedigree across three separate decades.

The tricolor diagonal sash design itself — instantly distinguishable from rivals Peñarol's horizontal stripes — is considered a classic of South American kit design and is popular with collectors purely for its heritage.

Shirts worn in the Clásico uruguayo against Peñarol, one of the oldest and most fiercely contested derbies in South American football, remain especially popular among Uruguayan collectors.

Nacional kits by decade

  • 1970s: Nacional won both the Copa Libertadores and the Intercontinental Cup in 1971, becoming South American and world champions in the same year.
  • 1980s: A second Intercontinental Cup arrived in 1980 against Nottingham Forest, followed by a third Copa Libertadores title in 1988.
  • 1990s-2000s: Nacional continued to compete domestically at the highest level, maintaining its rivalry with Peñarol as one of Uruguay's two dominant clubs.
  • 2010s-2020s: Nacional remains one of Uruguay's most successful and widely supported clubs, with the tricolor sash design continuing to define the shirt's identity.

Manufacturer records for Nacional's shirts across these decades are incomplete — we've focused this history on what's well documented rather than guess at kit suppliers by era.
