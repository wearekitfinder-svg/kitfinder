The most collectible Flamengo kits

The 1981 Intercontinental Cup shirt, worn by Zico when Flamengo demolished Liverpool 3-0 in Tokyo to become world champions, is the most iconic jersey in the club's history and among the most desired shirts in Brazilian football.

Zico's shirts throughout his career at the club during the late 1970s and early 1980s — a period widely considered Flamengo's golden generation — remain the most sought-after vintage pieces among collectors.

The 2019 Copa Libertadores final shirt, worn by Gabriel "Gabigol" Barbosa when he scored two goals in the final two minutes to complete an astonishing comeback against River Plate, defines the club's modern resurgence under Jorge Jesus.

Shirts from Flamengo's domestic Brasileirão title wins, including the historic 2019 season that combined league and continental success, are also popular among the club's enormous global fanbase.

Flamengo kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: Flamengo's shirts through the Zico era carried simple red-and-black designs that have become instantly recognisable symbols of Brazilian football's golden age.
  • 1990s-2000s: The club moved through a period of financial instability common to many Brazilian giants of the era, with shirts from various suppliers reflecting a less glamorous chapter.
  • 2010s: Adidas became Flamengo's long-term kit partner, worn through the club's return to domestic and continental prominence.
  • 2020s: Adidas remains Flamengo's supplier, continuing through the historic 2019 Libertadores/Brasileirão double under Jorge Jesus.
