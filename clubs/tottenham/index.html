The most collectible Tottenham kits

The 1987 FA Cup final shirt, worn by Glenn Hoddle and Chris Waddle in Spurs' distinctive Hummel kit with its bold diagonal design, is one of the most recognisable and sought-after shirts of the era.

The 1991 FA Cup final shirt — Paul Gascoigne's shirt before his infamous injury in that same match — remains hugely popular among collectors who remember "Gazzamania" at its peak.

Jürgen Klinsmann's 1994-95 shirt, worn during his sensational debut season in English football that included his famous diving goal celebration, is a cult classic among Spurs collectors.

More recently, Harry Kane's shirts from the 2018-19 Champions League run, which ended in the all-English final against Liverpool in Madrid, are the standout modern jerseys.

Tottenham kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: Admiral's designs (1977-80) gave way to Le Coq Sportif (1980-85) and then Hummel (1985-91), producing the club's most visually distinctive era, including the 1987 FA Cup final shirt.
  • 1990s: Umbro took over kit production through the Klinsmann and Sheringham years, a period defined by flair despite limited silverware.
  • 2000s-2010s: Puma and then Under Armour (2012-17) produced Spurs kits through the club's rise under managers like Harry Redknapp and Mauricio Pochettino.
  • 2010s-2020s: Nike has supplied Tottenham's kit since 2017, worn through the 2018-19 Champions League final run and the move to the new Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.
