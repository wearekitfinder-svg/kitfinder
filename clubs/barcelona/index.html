The most collectible Barcelona kits

The 1991-92 Kappa home shirt — worn by Johan Cruyff's Dream Team when they won the European Cup at Wembley — is one of the most coveted football shirts in the world. Original examples in good condition regularly sell for over £200, with player-issue versions fetching considerably more.

The 1980s Meyba era is Barcelona's most sought-after period for shirt collectors. Meyba, a Catalan sportswear brand, produced elegant and simple designs that defined an era. Any Meyba Barcelona shirt is now considered highly collectible.

The 2010-11 Unicef Champions League shirt — worn in the 3-1 Wembley final victory over Manchester United — is the most popular modern Barcelona jersey. Lionel Messi wore it during arguably his greatest ever season.

Ronaldinho's Barcelona shirts from 2003-08 are widely collected. His 2004-05 season (when he won the Ballon d'Or) and 2005-06 (Champions League winners) shirts are the most desirable. Any shirt with his name on the back commands a significant premium.

Barcelona kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: Meyba supplied Barcelona with iconic, clean designs. These shirts are rare and highly valuable.
  • 1990s: Kappa took over and produced some of the most visually striking Barcelona shirts ever, including the 1992 European Cup kit.
  • Late 1990s-2000s: Nike became the kit supplier in 1998 and have remained so. The early Nike kits with the Rivaldo and Figo era are popular.
  • 2010s: The treble-winning 2014-15 kit and the 2010-11 Champions League shirt are the standout collector pieces from this decade.
