The most collectible River Plate kits

The 2018 Copa Libertadores final shirt, worn by Gonzalo Martínez when River beat bitter rivals Boca Juniors 3-1 after extra time in the famous final played at the Santiago Bernabéu in Madrid, is the definitive modern River jersey.

Shirts from River's 1986 and 1996 Copa Libertadores-winning squads are treasured by long-time fans, tying together generations of the club's continental success.

The 2015 Copa Libertadores final shirt, won against Tigres of Mexico under manager Marcelo Gallardo — who went on to become the club's most successful coach in history — is also widely collected.

Older shirts from River's storied history, including kits worn during the "Máquina" ("The Machine") teams of the 1940s celebrated for their attacking football, are prized by serious historians of the club.

River Plate kits by decade

  • 1980s-90s: Adidas produced River's kits through both the 1986 and 1996 Copa Libertadores triumphs, cementing the brand's long association with the club.
  • 2000s: River endured a rare relegation to the second division in 2011, making shirts from the seasons immediately before and after highly collectible among fans who lived through it.
  • 2010s: Under Marcelo Gallardo, River won the 2015 and 2018 Copa Libertadores titles, restoring the club to the top of South American football.
  • 2020s: Adidas remains River's kit supplier, continuing the partnership through the club's ongoing domestic and continental campaigns.
