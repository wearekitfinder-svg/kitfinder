The most collectible Atlético Madrid kits

The 1995-96 shirt, worn by Kiko and Josémi when Atlético won the league and cup double under Radomir Antić, is one of the most celebrated in the club's history and a favourite among long-time Atleti collectors.

Fernando Torres's early shirts from his breakthrough as a teenager in the early 2000s, made by Spanish manufacturer Kelme, are highly desirable — capturing the local hero before his move to Liverpool.

The 2013-14 La Liga title-winning shirt, worn in the season Diego Simeone's side ended a decade of Real Madrid/Barcelona dominance, is the definitive shirt of the club's modern era.

Shirts from the 2014 and 2016 Champions League final defeats to Real Madrid, both fiercely contested, remain popular among fans as symbols of the club's near-misses at the very top.

Atlético Madrid kits by decade

  • 1980s-90s: Adidas supplied Atlético's kit through much of this period, including the 1995-96 double-winning season.
  • Early 2000s: Kelme, a Spanish brand, produced Atlético's shirts around the 2000 relegation to the second division; Nike became kit supplier for the 2001-02 promotion-winning season and has remained the club's manufacturer ever since.
  • 2010s: Under long-standing supplier Nike, Diego Simeone rebuilt Atlético into a genuine title contender, culminating in the 2013-14 La Liga title.
  • 2020s: Nike remains Atlético's kit supplier, worn through the 2020-21 La Liga title, the club's most recent championship.
