The most collectible Boca Juniors kits

Diego Maradona's shirts from his emotional homecoming spell at Boca between 1995 and 1997, and his earlier 1981 season before his move to Barcelona, are among the most desirable shirts in Argentine football history.

Shirts from Boca's golden run of Copa Libertadores titles in 2000, 2001 and 2003, featuring Juan Román Riquelme's playmaking and Carlos Tevez's emergence, define the club's most successful modern era.

The 2000 Intercontinental Cup shirt, worn when Boca beat Real Madrid to become world champions, remains a landmark jersey for a club that has always measured itself against Europe's biggest names.

Older shirts associated with La Bombonera — the club's famously steep, box-shaped stadium whose name means "the chocolate box" — are treasured as symbols of one of football's most intimidating atmospheres.

Boca Juniors kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: Boca's shirts through Maradona's two spells at the club carried simple, iconic blue-and-gold designs with minimal branding, typical of the era.
  • 1990s: Adidas became a long-term kit partner during the club's rebuilding years under Carlos Bianchi's early tenure.
  • 2000s: The Riquelme-Tevez era delivered three Copa Libertadores titles (2000, 2001, 2003) and the 2000 Intercontinental Cup, Boca's peak modern period.
  • 2010s-2020s: Adidas remains Boca's kit supplier today, continuing through the club's ongoing rivalry with River Plate in the Superclásico.
