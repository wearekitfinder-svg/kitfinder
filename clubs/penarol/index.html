The most collectible Peñarol kits

Shirts from Peñarol's 1961 and 1966 Intercontinental Cup-winning teams, which beat European champions Benfica and Real Madrid respectively, rank among the most historically significant jerseys in South American football.

The club's early Copa Libertadores titles of 1960, 1961, 1962 and 1966 established Peñarol as one of the continent's first true footballing powerhouses, and shirts connected to that era are treasured by Uruguayan collectors.

The black-and-yellow striped design itself, largely unchanged in spirit since the early 20th century, is considered one of the most instantly recognisable kit patterns in world football and is popular with collectors for its heritage value alone.

Shirts worn in the Clásico uruguayo against city rivals Nacional — one of the fiercest and oldest derbies in South America — are also widely sought after by fans of Uruguayan football.

Peñarol kits by decade

  • 1960s: Peñarol's golden era, winning four Copa Libertadores titles and two Intercontinental Cups, established the club among the very best in world football at the time.
  • 1970s-80s: Further domestic and continental success followed, with the club remaining one of Uruguay's two dominant forces alongside Nacional.
  • 1990s-2000s: Peñarol continued to compete domestically and in the Copa Libertadores, maintaining the club's historic black-and-yellow identity through several kit suppliers.
  • 2010s-2020s: Peñarol remains one of Uruguay's most-supported clubs, with the historic striped shirt continuing largely unchanged in design spirit.
