The most collectible Manchester City kits

The 2011-12 shirt — worn by Sergio Agüero when he scored the last-minute goal against QPR that won City their first title in 44 years — is the definitive modern City collector's piece, forever tied to the "Agüerooooo" moment.

The 1990s "Brother" kits, sponsored by the electronics manufacturer during City's yo-yo years between divisions, are a nostalgic favourite among fans who remember supporting the club before the Abu Dhabi takeover transformed its fortunes.

The 2011 FA Cup final shirt, City's first major trophy in 35 years, marked the true turning point of the modern era and is popular among collectors who trace the club's rise from that win.

Shirts from City's Treble-winning 2022-23 season under Pep Guardiola, featuring Erling Haaland's record-breaking debut campaign, are increasingly sought after as the club's most decorated period in its history.

Manchester City kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: City wore kits from a range of suppliers during this period, including Umbro and Kappa, in largely unremarkable seasons on the pitch — making surviving examples relatively scarce.
  • 1990s: The "Brother" sponsorship years produced some of the most fondly remembered — if commercially unglamorous — City shirts, worn through relegation and promotion battles.
  • 2000s-2010s: Thomas Cook and then Etihad Airways became shirt sponsors as the club's fortunes changed dramatically after 2008; the 2011-12 title-winning shirt is the standout piece.
  • 2020s: Puma has supplied City's kit since 2019, worn through the Treble-winning 2022-23 season under Pep Guardiola.
