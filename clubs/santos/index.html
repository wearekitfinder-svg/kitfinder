The most collectible Santos kits

Shirts from Santos' back-to-back Intercontinental Cup wins in 1962 and 1963, when Pelé and teammates like Coutinho and Pepe beat Benfica and AC Milan respectively to become world champions, are the club's most historically important jerseys.

Pelé's shirts from throughout his Santos career remain the single most desired pieces in Brazilian football memorabilia, with any confirmed period-correct example commanding enormous prices among serious collectors.

Neymar's shirts from his breakthrough years at Santos between 2009 and 2013, before his transfer to Barcelona, are highly popular among younger collectors who watched his emergence as one of the world's most exciting attackers.

The 2011 Copa Libertadores-winning shirt, worn by Neymar in the first Santos title in the competition since the Pelé era, is the standout modern Santos jersey.

Santos kits by decade

  • 1950s-70s: Santos' simple all-white kit, worn throughout Pelé's career at the club, remained largely unchanged and is treasured for its historical association with the greatest player of that generation.
  • 1980s-2000s: Santos moved between various kit suppliers during a period without the sustained success of the Pelé era, though the club remained a major force in São Paulo state football.
  • 2010s: The Neymar years (2009-2013) and the 2011 Copa Libertadores title brought renewed global attention to the club, nicknamed "Peixe" (the Fish).
  • 2020s: Santos has continued with various kit partners in recent seasons, including a period back in Brazil's second division before returning to the top flight.
