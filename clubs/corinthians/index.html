The most collectible Corinthians kits

Shirts from the Democracia Corinthiana era (1982-84) — some famously printed with the word "DEMOCRACIA" across the chest as a protest against Brazil's dictatorship — are among the most historically significant jerseys in football, prized well beyond ordinary collector circles.

Sócrates' shirts from his time as captain remain the most desired vintage Corinthians pieces, tying the shirt to one of Brazilian football's most intellectually distinctive figures — a qualified doctor as well as a playmaker.

The 2012 FIFA Club World Cup shirt, worn when Corinthians beat Chelsea 1-0 in Japan to become world champions under Tite, is the club's greatest modern achievement and its most sought-after contemporary jersey.

Shirts from Corinthians' 2012 Copa Libertadores win, completed months before the Club World Cup triumph, are equally popular among fans of that era's team.

Corinthians kits by decade

  • 1980s: The Democracia Corinthiana shirts of 1982-84, worn by Sócrates, Wladimir and Casagrande, remain the most culturally significant kits in the club's history.
  • 1990s-2000s: Corinthians moved through a series of kit suppliers, and from 2004 to 2007 entered a high-profile and controversial partnership with Media Sports Investment (MSI), the London-based investment fund that financed the signings of Carlos Tevez and Javier Mascherano.
  • 2010s: Nike became Corinthians' long-term kit partner, worn through the historic 2012 Libertadores and Club World Cup double.
  • 2020s: Nike remains Corinthians' supplier today, continuing through the club's ongoing rivalry with São Paulo state neighbours Palmeiras and Santos.
