The most collectible Borussia Dortmund kits

The 1996-97 Champions League final shirt, worn by Karl-Heinz Riedle and Lars Ricken when Dortmund beat Juventus 3-1 in Munich, remains the club's greatest triumph and its most sought-after vintage jersey.

Shirts bearing the Continentale sponsor logo — Die Continentale, a German insurance company that sponsored Dortmund's shirt from 1986 to 1999 — are a recognisable thread through the club's kits of that era.

Jürgen Klopp's 2010-11 and 2011-12 back-to-back Bundesliga title-winning shirts, worn by Mario Götze, İlkay Gündoğan and a young Robert Lewandowski, defined the club's most celebrated modern era.

Marco Reus's shirts across his long career at the club, and the 2012-13 Champions League final shirt from the all-German Wembley final against Bayern Munich, are also widely collected.

Borussia Dortmund kits by decade

  • 1970s-80s: Puma supplied Dortmund's kit for much of this period, producing simple, bold yellow designs long before the club's modern commercial growth.
  • 1990s: Nike took over as kit supplier in 1990, in time for the historic 1997 Champions League triumph.
  • 2000s: Nike remained through a turbulent financial period for the club, which nearly went bankrupt in 2005 before recovering.
  • 2010s-present: Puma returned as kit supplier in 2012, worn through Klopp's title-winning teams and the 2013 Champions League final.
