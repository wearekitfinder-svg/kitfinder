The most collectible Napoli kits

The 1986-87 Scudetto-winning shirt, worn by Maradona in the season Napoli won their first ever Italian title, is the single most iconic shirt in the club's history and among the most collected in world football.

Maradona's shirts from the 1989-90 title-winning season, Napoli's second and last Scudetto before the modern era, are also highly prized, along with his 1989 UEFA Cup-winning jersey.

Napoli's kits from the Maradona years were produced by Ennerre (NR), a now largely forgotten Italian manufacturer that has become a name collectors specifically search for because of its association with that era.

The 2022-23 Scudetto shirt, made by EA7 Giorgio Armani and worn by Victor Osimhen and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in Napoli's first title win in 33 years, is the standout modern collector's piece.

Napoli kits by decade

  • 1980s: Ennerre (NR) supplied Napoli's kit throughout the Maradona era, including both Scudetto-winning seasons of 1987 and 1990.
  • 1990s-2000s: Napoli moved through several kit suppliers during a turbulent period for the club, including bankruptcy and relegation to the lower divisions in the early 2000s.
  • 2010s: Under coaches like Rafael Benítez and Maurizio Sarri, Napoli's shirts featured a return to consistent European competition.
  • 2020s: EA7 Emporio Armani, Giorgio Armani's sportswear label, has supplied Napoli's kit since 2019, worn through the historic 2022-23 title win.
